{# Group panels, shared by the homepage shelves and the /apps grid. A group tile (button[data-group], see tiles.html) unfolds its panel (#group-panel-): the panel is moved in after opts.anchorFor(tile) - the last tile of the tile's row - and it animates open (grid rows 0fr -> 1fr), so whatever follows slides down. One panel is open at a time. ?group= in the URL opens that group on load, and follows the open panel. #}
